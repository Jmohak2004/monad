# Ghar demo script

Two minutes. Portrait phone. Voiceover uses the product's words: dollars, a link, a passkey. The screens below are the live UI.

## 1. The problem — 10s

**On screen:** Landing. Headline "Send money home in seconds."

**Voiceover:** Sending money home still costs a few percent, and it still takes days. It should be a link.

## 2. Account — 20s

**On screen:** "Get started." Type a first name. Button reads "Create account." The line under it mentions Face ID, fingerprint, or screen lock.

**Voiceover:** You open Ghar and create an account with your first name. One tap. Your phone's Face ID or fingerprint. No password, and nothing to write down.

## 3. Amount — 25s

**On screen:** Dollar field. Tap $100. The fee table appears: Ghar is free and arrives in seconds. Wise and Western Union show their cost and what the family would get.

**Voiceover:** Type a hundred dollars, or tap it. Ghar is free, and it arrives in seconds. The same hundred through Wise or Western Union costs more, and takes longer.

## 4. Share — 25s

**On screen:** "Ready to share." Headline "$100 is on its way." Button "Send on WhatsApp."

**Voiceover:** Send. The link is ready. Share it on WhatsApp with the person you're paying. Only they should have it. If nobody opens it within seven days, you can take it back.

## 5. Receive — 30s

**On screen:** Claim page. "Someone sent you" and the amount. Then a passkey, then "You received" and "No fees were taken."

**Voiceover:** They tap the link. They don't install an app. They create an account the same way, with a passkey, and the money is in their balance. No fees were taken.

## 6. Close — 10s

**On screen:** Hold on "You received," or return to the landing headline.

**Voiceover:** No app. No seed phrase. No gas. Just a link.
